FireOps Mobile v1.7

App feature list

Workflow Edition for wildfire contractors, employees, fleet teams, and company administrators.

iPhone Android Web / Mac simulator Contractor experience Employee experience

01 Operational dashboard

  • Live command-center view of incidents, personnel, resources, and alerts
  • Metric drilldowns for assignments and items needing attention
  • Required-action queue and recent operational activity
  • NIFC daily situation report, preparedness level, resource summary, and geographic-area fire activity
  • Quick actions for availability, gear checkout, evaluations, payroll, receipts, and invoices

02 Incident map and field operations

  • Interactive incident map with configurable map layers
  • Official wildfire incident and National Weather Service alert connections with fallback demo data
  • Incident acreage, containment, personnel, and source details
  • Assigned resources, personnel, readiness, crew size, and open-issue views
  • Issue reporting from incident workspace with fleet-record links

03 Fleet and maintenance

  • Searchable vehicle and apparatus records
  • Mileage, engine-hour, assignment, readiness, and open-issue tracking
  • Digital equipment binder with photos and PDF attachments
  • Post-assignment inspections with checklist results, notes, photos, and electronic signatures
  • Automatic issue creation for inspection defects and critical out-of-service failures
  • Standalone issue reports with priority, description, and reporter details

04 Crew communications

  • Company, incident, unit, and maintenance chat threads
  • New-thread creation with thread type and starting members
  • Urgent-message labeling and acknowledgement status
  • Communication and marketplace notification preferences

05 Qualifications and incident cards

  • Qualification Vault for credentials, certificates, task books, licenses, and annual records
  • Photo, image-library, and PDF evidence uploads
  • Incident experience records with supporting documents
  • RT-130 refresher and Work Capacity Test currency tracking
  • IQS-style consolidated master record and PDF packet preparation
  • Incident Qualification Card builder with live preview, trainee positions, branding, verification, and export
  • Credential expiration and renewal visibility

06 Workforce and marketplace

  • Portable worker profiles with resume, experience, qualifications, availability, and travel preferences
  • Privacy controls for opt-in profile visibility and sensitive records
  • Qualified-worker search and candidate contact workflows
  • Wildland job board, job posting, interest tracking, and applicant inbox
  • Equipment, rental, parts, and service listings with photos, price, location, and status
  • Marketplace search, seller messaging, listing editing, and sold/active controls

07 Inventory accountability

  • Available-gear counts with administrator editing
  • Gear checkout by item, quantity, and employee assignment
  • Currently checked-out inventory view
  • Gear check-in and employee accountability tracking

08 Invoices and finance

  • Invoice overview with outstanding, paid, draft, sent, and ready statuses
  • Branded invoice builder with billing details, net terms, and line items
  • PDF generation and invoice-overview storage
  • AI-assisted invoice import from PDF or camera with editable field review
  • Current-fiscal-year QuickBooks sync and export-ready actions
  • Private payroll-form capture, AI total detection, submission, and administrator review queue
  • Receipt photography and categorized receipt capture

09 Company roster and training

  • Company roster with real-time available/unavailable status
  • Employee call, text, credential view, share, and PDF export actions
  • Training course catalog, registration, capacity, and registrant tracking
  • Administrator course publishing
  • Official wildland-learning schedule link and registration notifications
  • Employee evaluation capture from camera or photo library

10 Documents and company setup

  • Company document library with role-aware upload access
  • PDF, image, and Word document publishing, viewing, downloading, and sharing
  • Company logo, representative signature, phone, and representative-name branding
  • Contractor and employee account experiences in one app
  • Feature controls for Marketplace, Training Center, and customizable hub layout

11 Users, permissions, and integrations

  • User invitations, role templates, and individual permission overrides
  • Access controls for operations, fleet, payroll, receipts, reports, integrations, and administration
  • Master-admin full-access model
  • QuickBooks Online, Motive Fleet, and cloud-sync connection screens
  • Server-side credential and audit-trail security model for production integrations

12 Personalization and assistant

  • Customizable FireOps Hub with long-press and drag tile ordering
  • Saved on-device hub layout
  • Floating FireOps Assistant available throughout app
  • Permission-aware guidance to app workflows
  • SMS invitation composer for onboarding crew members
  • Compact high-resolution brand header and mobile-safe navigation spacing

Prototype scope

  • Current build uses local and demonstration data.
  • Real push delivery, cloud accounts, OCR/AI services, PDF file output, messaging, printing, QuickBooks synchronization, and role enforcement require production backend and native-service configuration.
  • Qualification records and cards are contractor-issued FireOps records, not official agency IQS/IQCS records or Red Cards.
